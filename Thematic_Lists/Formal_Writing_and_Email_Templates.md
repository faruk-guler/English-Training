## 📧 Örnek Resmî E-posta ve Yazışma Şablonları (Templates)

Aşağıdaki şablonları iş başvurularında, şikâyetlerde, bilgi taleplerinde ve iş hayatında doğrudan kullanabilirsiniz.

### 1. İş Başvurusu ve Ön Yazı (Job Application / Cover Letter)
**Konu (Subject):** Application for [Pozisyon Adı] - [Adınız Soyadınız]

```text
Dear Hiring Manager, [veya Dear Mr./Ms. Soyadı]

I am writing to express my strong interest in the [Pozisyon Adı] position currently available at [Şirket Adı], as advertised on [İlanın Görüldüğü Platform].

With my background in [Uzmanlık Alanınız] and [Süre] years of experience in [İlgili Sektör], I have developed strong skills in [1-2 Temel Beceri]. In my previous role at [Önceki Şirket], I successfully [Elde Edilen Somut Bir Başarı/Proje].

I am very excited about the opportunity to contribute to [Şirket Adı]'s continued success. Please find my resume attached for your review.

Thank you for your time and consideration. I look forward to the possibility of discussing my application with you further.

Sincerely,
[Adınız Soyadınız]
[Telefon Numaranız]
[LinkedIn Profil Linkiniz]
```

---

### 2. Şikâyet ve İade Talebi (Customer Complaint & Refund Request)
**Konu (Subject):** Complaint Regarding Order #[Sipariş No] - Defective Product / Request for Refund

```text
Dear Customer Support Team,

I am writing to file a formal complaint regarding my recent purchase of [Ürün Adı] (Order #[Sipariş No]), placed on [Sipariş Tarihi].

Upon receiving the package on [Teslim Alma Tarihi], I noticed that [Sorunun Tanımı - örn: the item was damaged / the product does not match the description on your website]. I have attached photographs of the damaged item along with a copy of my invoice for your reference.

As this item is unusable, I kindly request a full refund to my original payment method [veya: a prompt replacement].

I would appreciate it if you could confirm receipt of this email and inform me of the next steps at your earliest convenience.

Kind regards,
[Adınız Soyadınız]
[İletişim Bilgileriniz]
```

---

### 3. Bilgi İsteme ve Teklif Talebi (Inquiry & Request for Proposal - RFP)
**Konu (Subject):** Inquiry Regarding [Hizmet veya Ürün Adı] - [Şirketiniz / Adınız]

```text
Dear [Muhatabın Adı / Sales Team],

I hope this email finds you well.

I am writing on behalf of [Şirketiniz / Şahsınız] to inquire about your [Hizmet veya Ürün Adı]. We are currently planning to [Projenizin veya İhtiyacınızın Kısa Özeti] and are considering your solutions.

Could you please provide us with more details regarding:
1. Pricing options and licensing models
2. Implementation timelines
3. Available customer support packages

If possible, we would like to schedule a brief 20-minute call next week to discuss this further. 

Thank you in advance for your assistance.

Best regards,
[Adınız Soyadınız]
[Unvanınız]
[Şirketiniz]
```

---

### 4. Toplantı Talep Etme ve Planlama (Meeting Request & Scheduling)
**Konu (Subject):** Meeting Request: Discussing [Konu Başlığı]

```text
Dear [Kişinin Adı],

I hope you are having a productive week.

I would like to schedule a meeting with you to discuss [Toplantı Konusu / Proje Adı]. We need to review [Görüşülecek Temel 1-2 Madde] before moving forward with the next stage.

Would you be available for a 30-minute call on either [1. Tarih ve Saat] or [2. Tarih ve Saat]? If neither of these times works for you, please let me know a date and time that fits your schedule.

Looking forward to your reply.

Best regards,
[Adınız Soyadınız]
[Unvanınız]
```

---

### 5. Özür ve Durum Düzeltme (Formal Apology & Rectification)
**Konu (Subject):** Apology for the Delay Regarding [Proje / Sipariş / Rapor Adı]

```text
Dear [Kişinin Adı / Client Name],

Please accept our sincere apologies for the unexpected delay in delivering [Rapor / Proje / Teslimat Adı]. 

Unfortunately, due to [Gecikmenin Kısa ve Makul Sebebi - örn: unforeseen technical difficulties / supply chain disruptions], we were unable to meet the agreed deadline of [Orijinal Teslim Tarihi]. 

We have resolved the issue and are pleased to confirm that your delivery will be completed by [Yeni Kesin Tarih]. We deeply value our partnership and are taking strict measures to ensure this does not recur.

Thank you for your understanding and continued patience.

Sincerely,
[Adınız Soyadınız]
[Unvanınız]
```

### 6. Resmi Teşekkür ve Takip (Thank You & Follow-Up)
**Konu (Subject):** Thank You - [Görüşülen Konu / Mülakat / Toplantı] Follow-Up

```text
Dear [Kişinin Adı],

Thank you very much for taking the time to meet with me today to discuss [Toplantı / Mülakat Konusu]. I greatly enjoyed our conversation and learning more about [Bahsedilen Önemli Bir Nokta].

Our discussion reinforced my enthusiasm for [Pozisyon / Ortaklık / Proje]. As discussed, I will send over the requested documents by tomorrow afternoon.

Please let me know if you need any additional information from my side.

Warm regards,
[Adınız Soyadınız]
[İletişim Bilgileriniz]
```
